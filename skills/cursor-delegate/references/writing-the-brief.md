# Writing the brief

State the outcome, workspace, allowed paths, constraints, and runnable checks. Tell the implementer
who owns commits and publication. Give the reviewer the paths and evidence to inspect. Put permission
restrictions in relay flags as well as the brief.

Ask for a concise final report with the changes, checks run and anything unresolved. When working
through Agent Orchestrator, include its required `orch-result` block. Resume with just the new
instruction or correction. Cursor keeps the earlier turns in its chat store.
